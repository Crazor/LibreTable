# 0009: Non-profit association and accessibility partners

- **Status:** proposed
- **Date:** 2026-11-26
- **Owner:** Architect

## Context

Some funders (e.g. Aktion Mensch) and Apple's developer fee waiver require a non-profit organization; screen-reader play needs testing with blind and visually impaired players.

## Decision (proposal)

Evaluate in 2027: a registered association with a real board, or a partner organization (e.g. a regional association of the blind) as applicant and test partner.

## Consequences

- Tax-privileged purposes would rest on help for people with disabilities, education and care for the elderly rather than on card games themselves (to be checked by a tax adviser).
- Apple's fee waiver reportedly excludes in-app purchases, which conflicts with the tip model (to verify).

## Open points

- Timing, partners, tax review.
